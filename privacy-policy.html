Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Data collection

Travelingo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. No account is required to use the app.

Local storage

All user progress (completed lessons, quiz scores) is stored locally on your device using IndexedDB. This data never leaves your device.

Analytics

Travelingo does not use any analytics or tracking service.

Third-party services

Travelingo does not integrate any third-party SDK that collects personal data.

Content

Language learning packs are downloaded from GitHub Pages (https://pascalheraud.github.io/travelingo/) and cached locally on your device. No user data is sent during this download.

Contact
